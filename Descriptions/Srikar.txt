Srikar V. Srinivas is a postdoctoral researcher at Idaho National Laboratory (INL), where he works in the Systems Science and Engineering division under the Energy and Environment Science & Technology (EES&T) directorate. He holds a bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering from Anna University, India, and earned his Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the National University of Singapore (NUS). His doctoral work focused on machine learning-enabled dashboards for chemical equipment maintenance, box-constrained optimization, design of experiments, and piecewise modeling. After completing his Ph.D. in 2023, he continued at NUS as a Research Fellow, investigating the techno-economic impact of catalysts in green hydrogen production from ammonia for power generation. At INL, Srikar has contributed to projects spanning generative AI, techno-economic analysis, mixed-integer linear programming, biomass and waste valorization, and critical materials.
