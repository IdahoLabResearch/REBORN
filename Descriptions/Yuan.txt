Yuan-Yuan Lee, PhD has an extensive academic background in data analysis, political science, and social science. Specializing in simulation modeling, which includes agent-based models, system dynamics, and machine learning, she has applied her expertise to a diverse range of topics such as social economics, human behavior, recycling, and urban planning. Additionally, she has gained valuable experience in government consulting in Taiwan, particularly in the area of public and private partnerships (PPP) projects. Her main focus areas include land use, project finance evaluation, public engagement, and contracting.
