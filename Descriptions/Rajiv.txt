Rajiv Paudel is a systems modeling researcher at Idaho National Laboratory (INL) whose work focuses on developing sustainable supply systems for bioenergy generation in the United States. He designs, develops, and maintains advanced modeling frameworks to support complex energy system decision-making, using approaches such as geospatial modeling, agent-based modeling, and discrete event simulation. Through these methods, he analyzes supply chain dynamics, resource availability, infrastructure planning, and overall system performance to help advance resilient, efficient, and sustainable bioenergy systems.
